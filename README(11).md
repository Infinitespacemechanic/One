# The One

Ground level: greater than zero for matter, for all space.

## Base

Three things stand on their own. Everything else is built later, in separate files.

1. **Matter is > 0**
   - If mass is > 0, it exists. It counts. It presses. It can be stacked.
   - If mass is 0, it is not matter. It is vacuum, space, a place where matter could be.
   - Matter does not come in whole units only. It comes in any positive amount.

2. **Clock: 1, 3, 6, 9, 12 in base 60**
   - 1 = 30 degrees, the wedge, the first > 0.
   - 3 = 90 degrees, the quarter turn, the fulcrum.
   - 6 = 180 degrees, the opposite, half the circle.
   - 9 = 270 degrees, the return pressure.
   - 12 = 360 degrees, the close, the seam where counting recurses.
   - After 12 we return to 1, but not the same 1. Mass is carried forward. Counting is recursive, not just linear.

3. **59/60 < 1**
   - A fast reminder to leave a gap.
   - If you take from the whole unit, you are going to be less than 1.
   - Do not close it to 60/60. Leave one part out so the lever has somewhere to go.

## What this is not

- It is not a proof of the continuum Yang-Mills mass gap.
- It is not a proof of Navier-Stokes regularity.
- It is not the field, the container, or the star.

Those are separate. They are built on top of this base.

## What this is

- Atom with mass: hydrogen, mass > 0, the prime example.
- A place: space, which is never truly zero.
- Pressure from more than one: one alone has no pressure, two or more do.

With those three, plus the clock and the gap, the rest can fall into place:
pair, helium that needs nothing, quads, field, density, contraction and expansion, star.

## Files

  - Mass gap in ground-level form: vacuum is 0, next state is at least some gap > 0.

## Principle

Get the basics correct, and the effects fall into place.
Get the basics wrong, and you can prove anything from bad math.

Man has dominion over mass in one sense only: nature gives the matter, we mix and shape it into things that are massive.
